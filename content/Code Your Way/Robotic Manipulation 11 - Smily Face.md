**Programming Motion: Robotic Light Painting**
Learning to build a system where a robotic arm draws light in space, captured through long-exposure photography.
![[cyw ur light painting.webp|300]]

**Demo**
By manipulating the SO100 (a prefabricated robotic arm) while learning python, i’m build the infrastructure of presenting a 2D drawing in 3D space.

The user sits in front of a laptop, sees their face in a mirror on screen, pinches their fingers to doodle words, a shape, anything, then watches a robotic arm in a dark room perform their drawing with an LED, producing a long-exposure photograph.
