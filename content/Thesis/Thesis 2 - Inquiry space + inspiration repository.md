The ask:
> - Inquiry space. Look at your concept map from class. What are your key areas of interest? These can be fields, themes, media, techniques. Draw a diagram describing your inquiry space. The idea is that it serve as a map, helping guide your research and, later, your creative experiments. [Here are some examples](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1iekg8RLOjlneS7dyTTM6q6g1-h-UvfTXt_LvRsBEMFc/edit#heading=h.yr2mnaic61kd).
    Start building your repository for inspiration. For your areas of interest: what terms are used, who are the people working in this field, where do you find their work, what looks interesting? Here are some suggestions on how to find sources to add to your list: 
> - Browse in the library, a bookstore, looking for relevant books, journal articles, and other media. Keep track of what you find, following a format like [this](https://cezar-mocan.notion.site/Academic-Research-6c9c576657534c81803cd5a069d3c976) (more thorough), or like [this one](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fcFX7gOiM11PHAeUC1tnoQKTywQltiLlsEqvU3rk3aA/edit?usp=sharing) (more lightweight).
> - Browse online, talk to peers. Find relevant media – artworks, films, games, exhibitions, concerts, talks, documentaries, podcasts, others – and add them, too.
> - Browse the ITP [Thesis Archives](https://itp.nyu.edu/thesis/archive/), find 2 or 3 projects that resonate with you. Watch their presentations.

## ITP Thesis Archive

I did not find any robot related projects in the archive but I know of 2 meaningful projects i've seen coming from ITP:
1. [[Peeqo]] - Peeqo is a voice activated desktop companion that responds entirely through GIFs. Made byy itp alum Abhishek Singh. 
2. [[Tweenbots]] - Tweenbots are human-dependent cardboard robots that navigate the city with the help of pedestrians they encounter. 

Both project address human robot interaction by creating small scale robots that interact with people surrounding them. Peeqo is more of a desktop friend using GIFs as a communication platform, it is fun and whimsical. Tweenbots need the help of the people surrounding them, they have an objective to get to a specific point but they need help getting there - don't we all?
