Kawaii is a Japanese cultural aesthetic that ==celebrates childlike cuteness, charm, and innocence==.

Core Characteristics
- **Visual features:** Large eyes, round faces, small mouths, and soft or pastel color palettes.
- **Emotional impact:** Designed to evoke warmth, comfort, and nurturing feelings.
- **Global reach:** Originating in Japan and popularized by icons like [Sanrio's Hello Kitty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawaii), it spans fashion, art, and lifestyle.
