### What if the shop could talk back?

In present times it feels like the amount of possabilities is growing expedentiolay, we are living in a time that an ai process can think create and do so much for ourselves. It feels too good to be true and like we are running in the fastest speed ever. Never the less, new research suggests that students are doing all their work with ai and are not getting anything to their brain, no one knows exactly what the future holds and how this new technology will influence, but STEM teaching is key and learning by doing is key and so far ai and robotics cant replace that notion, if anything they can contribute!

What if the shop could talk back? Is a research about human robot interaction added as a supportive layer to a maker space accomponiying different stake holders. Researched at 2 different maker shops with similar kinds of communities our project aims to give rods not fish to users who feel unsecure using heavy machinery that can facilitate their STEM journey. Our projects starts as an animated robotic helper to students in undergrad and masters degree and can grow to be an entity helping in different maker spaces environments, we imagine a robot helping children in school maker spaces, or different people in public maker spaces, giving a hand and supporting questions that arouse while creating and making. In the itp ima shop and tandem maker space you would expect mature students hwo know how to operate these kind of machines, actoually different personas are regulated, from new comers that have never had hand on experience with physical creation to more savvy users (that dont remember how to use the machines), the robot should be able to help guide or facilitate the interaction with different kind of users opn different levels. 

The interaction between humans and machines in the shop environment can be frustrating, the user might have gone through a training but dont remember how to use the machine, a shop staff is helpful but might not be able to give a full attention to the learning process which is a key part of using a machine, furthermore the different stages of creating a physical object grows and teaches the user to overcome challenges software based creation will never have. When getting to the laser machine a normal user have used the machine once or just has seen someone using it while training, they are uncertain at the stages and unfameliar with the program and setup. It requires multi step approach (computer design for the illustrator file, then uploading to specific program and pressing many different buttons, knowing which materials are allowed and forbidden to use and knowing what is a good cut etc).

We imagine a physical embodied robot at this situation, using an open source platform - the reachy mini we are proposing giving the shop a way to perceive its space and communicate how to use the machines. The robot was added with some embedded profile features and a specific persona - it is a socratic teacher intended of helping students get agency and feel like they can do things on their own (show research about how using ai is making ppl have less choices and just approve the next prompt instead of actoually learning). 

Design goals - as 2 master degree students working in the itp shop and tandem maker space we have noticed how many times students from different background come to seek help because they dont know how to use machines or dont feel empowered to do so alone, we also acknowledge that the physical creation process is a skill to learn (exmpale how to build a box from plywood using the laser machine). Socially we believe that learning to create while altering hardware is a great skill that gives ppl agency of controlling the world, coming form industrial design background i sometimes feel like having a super power, i can get up in the morning imagining a physical product, few hours later i can have something in my hand that matches that dream. Physically we wanted to enchant the maker space with a character users feel comfortable talking to it, it isnt the deux ex machina, its not the voice of god coming from no where, but an embodied personified robot, unthretening, helpful. Biggest ethical constraints and biggest issue is unwanted surveillance be bystanders, people who never chose to encounter the robot and are in the background of the interaction. For them and this scenario we introduced an addon to the reachy mini in the form of a visor covering its camera. The robot can be controlled by voice and answer questions but cant see unless given a tactile gift of the user raising its visor to give the camera the ability to precivre space - and look at what the user is doing and help in this situation (“hi reachy (ari) look at this cut i made, what is wrong? Or “hi ari, i dont remember where is the machines vacuum suction on button can you look and tell me where that is? (for agent - this is actoually a bad example because the robot can also rembebr or have that knowledge as part of its context, so we need to find something else). Practical constraints  
Why not each student use their own ai from phone - in its first layer of knowledge, without needing to go online ari has everything needed about each machine in the shop, moreover we gave it the specific knowledge gathered by shop staff to understand what are practices used in the specific shop (shop staff most approve the material used, shop staff most look at the file before using the machine to look for areas that might create a fire etc), this gives ari the ability to lead students until a specific time and then make a security handoff to the needed personal. Moreover - by having a physical agent facilitating the shop - we can give it a personality. From our experience with the interaction design of llms (ship, make decisions without asking the user or trying to get to the best outcome without learning from the way there) we wanted to reassure and give agency to the users, therefor we made the persona of a socratic teacher, asking questions and suggesting - not telling exactly what to do, this is connected to a greater notion we have for the future, how will people learn new skills in a world giving everything on a silver platter? Experienced ppl can use ai to do exactly what they want, if touve coded for years you know to tell the ai what are the irritating stages you wnat it to code for you while you control the architecture. For newcomers, ppl learning how to code while using ai - they only see the front front end - they write prompt without knowing the loops inside the code - this is to show that whoever is in the decisions ranks of the community (and whoever is reading and ranking this paper) might not know how students are using ai.

design process and rationale - here we will discuss some tacit research dont - we started by giving ppl the opportunity (or we watched ppl?) to just use their agent on their phone while using the laser machine (why we choose the laser machine?), the nwe gave them an ai trained on the machinery and shop knowledge, then we gave the same agent with a socratic teacher persona, then we encapsulated everything to a reachy mini - and the users were much more happy with it.

  

I want to add a comparison of the robot to existing makerspace safety systems: card access versus a social contract, in the way ppl are using the machines and the space, theres a question if being watched gives you more ability or scares a user, this is why the robot have a visor.

Future outlook: Align with the theme of public spaces

Robots are coming, there is a gap between how good ai agents are and our seamless interaction with them (new models for ongoing speech and video interaction) and how hardware is (the difference between how good ai agent talk and how many steps behind is the physical actuated robot is).

Before we can facilitate them in our public spaces we need to get used to them and to see how they can help us. Maybe an embodied ai character can smoothly get us ready to the next leap when technology will start to walk and drive around us. Moreover - thei interaction design around ai is lacking guardrails, we dont know how will this new tech influence younger generations, be giving them fish they for sure wont be better at solving complex problems, by teaching in a socratic way - they might.

Working on this project has encouraged us to examine how does the gap between the all knowing ai agent can reflect in a real world public space scenario, we choose places that facilitate a community that is broadly adapting ai agents, but is still growing (undergards) and learning about how to create in this world.**
