## Shared-space entries already in 2026
Brick by Brick (social isolation in public spaces) · HighLight (shared spaces) · Elevator Pitch (architectural robots) · Peri (retrofitted public infrastructure, in the wild) · Data Chase (museum) · Robot Housekeeper Cart (hotel etiquette) · MERRY (community robot).
So "a robot in a shared space" was common even before it became the theme. In 2027 every entry will be in a public space. Being on theme won't distinguish you.

## Gaps in the 2026 titles
No title is about privacy, consent, what a robot perceives, or who owns it. No makerspace entry. Two tutoring/learning entries (RoboTales, Coached Yoga), neither in a shared space.
That's where your novelty is: **perception and ownership made public through the body**, in a space nobody has used.

## What the winners seem to do (from titles, your reading, and the call)
- A single object with one clear idea, named memorably (Bickering Benches, Beckoning Bowl).
- A point of view: the benches are designed from the bench's side. You noticed this.
- They describe a plan and a design, not study results.
- A physical thing people can touch at the showcase (benches were shipped from Germany).
- The rubric adds: concrete multi-party design and honest failure modes.
