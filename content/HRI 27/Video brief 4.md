# Video brief 4: title, gap, three degrees, how it works, the room

Replaces [[Video brief 3]] for structure. It follows the five sections Aram and Aditya proposed (13/11), with sections 3 and 4 merged and two sections added. Look and sound, paper stills and the shoot checklist from [[Video brief 2]] still apply. The format is still ELEGNT's: short labeled scenes, informative, no voiceover selling it.

## Why it changed from Brief 3
[[Paper Draft 7]] moved the centre of the paper to **degrees of Socraticness** (contribution 1). Interaction design for a shared room is now contribution 2, and the prototype is contribution 3. Brief 3 spent most of its time on the perception states. Brief 4 follows the paper's order of importance: how much Ari tells, then how it works, then the room.

## One sentence
A robot in a shared makerspace should tell newcomers just enough that they finish the job themselves, without taking their place or watching them.
Closing line (from the [[Paper brief]]): "The measure of a teaching robot is what people can do when it's gone."

### On-screen text for section 2 (two lines, one at a time)
1. "Staff answer the same laser questions all night. Newcomers wait, or ask a phone that tells them which button to press."
2. "We built Ari, a Socratic tutor for the makerspace."

Frame it as experience, not findings. We haven't run EXP 3, so nothing on screen may imply we measured the gap.

### Captions for section 4
1. "A realtime voice model answers at once."
2. "A slower reasoning model reads the conversation and the shop's handbook, and passes a note to the next reply."
3. "Safety rules live in the fast model, so a hazard never waits a turn."
Add the latency only if EXP 0 has a median by the edit: "Median response: [x] s." **No number, no claim.** Don't use the word "solved."

## Rules for the edit
- Don't make staff look like they're failing. They're busy, and they send her to Ari. The paper is on their side and they will watch this.
- Title cards: one or two words, one plain typeface, same position each time.
- No voiceover. Captions on all dialogue. Music only under sections 1 and 4, never under speech.
- Her hands do every step. Ari never touches the machine.
- If over 3:00, cut from section 4 first (to 15 s, captions 1 and 3 only), then **Handing over**. Never cut **Asking**, **Anyone can close** or the ending.

## Cut from Brief 3
**Resting** (folded into the title and the ending), **Seeing** (the visor-open POV now appears only inside **Anyone can close**, as the before), **Not telling** (one line in the paper is enough), the montage of other people (no time; the Tandon makerspace goes into stills for the paper).
