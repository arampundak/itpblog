# Video brief 2: a possible near future, not a product ad

Replaces [[Video brief 1]] in tone. Structure and logistics still apply.

## Reference: Little Signals (Google Seed Studio with Map, 2022)
https://experiments.withgoogle.com/little-signals
Six small objects, each a study in one way to signal softly, moving between background and foreground. I couldn't play the video here, so watch it together and point at what you like: pace, framing, sound, how text is used.
What to borrow: each object demonstrates **one signal**, clearly, then moves on. It feels made now, but it shows a possible future.
What to add that they don't have: **other people in frame**. Our claim is about the room, so the signals must be read by someone who isn't the user.

## The idea for him in two lines
A small robot lives in a university shop and helps newcomers at the laser cutter. It shows everyone what it hears and sees, anyone can close its eye, and it teaches the rules without telling on anyone.

## Look and sound
Natural shop light, steady camera (tripod or slow gimbal), real ambient sound low under dialogue, captions always. Title cards in one plain typeface. No music under speech. The robot is never the only thing in frame for long.

## For the paper
Shoot each vignette also as a still: these become Figure 1 (hero) and Figure 2 (perception states).

## Shoot checklist
Releases for everyone visible · staff permission and a staff member present while the laser runs · cast: user, waiting student, neighbor, staff · lav mic + clean robot audio · someone off-camera puppets the head turn and wake · 2 days (Nov 14–15), one per site · rough cut by Nov 18.
