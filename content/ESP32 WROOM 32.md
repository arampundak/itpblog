The ESP32-WROOM-32 is ==a popular, powerful, and generic Wi-Fi+BT+BLE MCU module designed by Espressif==, featuring a dual-core CPU up to 240 MHz, 4 MB SPI flash, and a PCB antenna. It is widely used in IoT applications, ranging from low-power sensor networks to voice encoding, and is compatible with the Arduino IDE.
