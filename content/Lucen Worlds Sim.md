[Lucen Worlds](https://lucenworlds.com/) is an ==interactive web-based physics and robotics simulation platform== where you can play with and test trained robot policies in real-time browser environments.
![[ref - lucen worlds 1.webp]]

Lucen Worlds was created by **Lucen Robotics**, a startup co-founded by **Linyan Fu** and **Apheth D. Almeida**.

![[ref - lucen worlds 2.webp]]

Backed by the startup accelerator [Founders, Inc.](https://f.inc/canopy/teams/lucen-robotics/), the team originally focused on autonomous retail restocking robots. They later pivoted to build an **open-source, modular humanoid robotics developer kit**. [[1](https://f.inc/canopy/teams/lucen-robotics/), [2](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/launch-fund-ai-meets-robotics/lucen-robotics/lucen-robotics), [3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o91cw9Uus8A), [4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gXy-y6LnEaE&vl=en-US), [5](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/lucen-robotics)]

The Lucen Worlds web simulator acts as a digital companion to their physical hardware. It allows developers and hobbyists to test reinforcement learning policies in simulation before deploying them onto the physical modular robot builds (such as their biped robot, _Laika v2_).
