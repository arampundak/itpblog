MDA framework: Mechanics --> Dynamics --> Aesthetics
Mechanics - rules and components. in basketball: the ball, the court, the hoops, a rule is dribble to move with ball.
Dynamics - interaction between players and the mechanics. behavior the emerges as a result of the rules. Dribbling behind the back is a dynamic.
Aesthetics - feelings experienced throughout the gameplay. what is the mood and vibe of the game.  Fast paced, cooperative and competitive.

"Thinking, Fast and Slow"
System 1 - Automatic thinking: simple math equations, making a facial expression, differences in size. 
System 2 - Effortful Thinking: solve complex calculations, emotional regulation, fill out a tax form.

Cognitive Bias is systematic error in thinking which can negatively affect out decision making. A mental shortcut or heuristic

Confirmation Bias - you are biased for an example and you think everything supports that. you don't want to feed your players with what you think is correct. you want people to try your way, the instinct we have to confirm what we believe is the right thing.
