## Full text

Ever been stuck on CAD, can't quite figure out how to get those surfaces to blend properly, or features just keep breaking. Here are my go to YouTube channels to help solve problems and push my CAD skills.

Since I use SOLIDWORKS for the majority of the things I do, I'd love for everyone to share their go to when stuck on CAD problems across all softwares.

Andrew Jackson (AJ design studio)
https://lnkd.in/eRuXkDVU

Advanced surface modeling techniques, videos are fast and straight to the point, they also showcase some really good modeling practices and modeling structure and the importance of planning a model.


 Forge Product Development LLC
https://lnkd.in/eJc3FJ_D

Great videos and lots of different CAD development, they run through real problems submitted by people which is a great way to work through problems.

3D modeling STEM
https://lnkd.in/eNa2EmDy

This channel has lots of videos covering all different softwares and CAD modeling packages, great and easy learning. Especially if you want to pick up a new package.

DiMonte Group Inc.
https://lnkd.in/eG7W9v6h

A treasure trove of different videos ranging from entire series about fillets to great modeling practices.

Easy cad solutions
https://lnkd.in/eTr6CdCM

They post some really unique videos and use solidworks in an unconventional way that could translate well across all types of CAD

Did I miss anything, drop a comment. If you liked this and these resources help let me know.
