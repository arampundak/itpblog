These humanoid robots run on the world's smallest robotic servo joints, according to LT Cyber Labs.

LT Cyber Labs built these 24 inch miniature humanoid robots that can move similar to a full-size humanoid.

The miniature humanoids are powered by their micro digital servos, built entirely in-house, and what they tout as the world's smallest robotic servo joints.
