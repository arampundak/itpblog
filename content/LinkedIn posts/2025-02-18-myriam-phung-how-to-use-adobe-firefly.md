How to use Adobe Firefly to create stunning mockups?

Here’s my go-to method:
1 Start by creating a reference visual with simple shapes and bold, contrasting colors.
2 Write a precise and detailed prompt in Adobe Firefly that matches the image you’re envisioning.
3 Upload your visual (created in Illustrator) as a “reference image” and the AI-generated image as “reference style.”
 Adjust the “reference style” or repeat the process if the results need refining.
4 Download your final image and polish it in Photoshop by adding your design to complete the mockup.

 Pro Tip: Experiment with different prompts and reference styles to achieve unique results!
