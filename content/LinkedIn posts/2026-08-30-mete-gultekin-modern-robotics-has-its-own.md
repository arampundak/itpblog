## Full text

Modern robotics has its own family tree.

It starts with Willow Garage. Several companies founded by its alumni were acquired by Google in 2013, helping form Google's early robotics effort.

Years later, parts of Google's robotics and AI research came together under Google DeepMind. That talent pool then branched out again through the founders of Physical Intelligence, Generalist and Dyna Robotics.

The same pattern continues around companies such as Figure and Skild AI: people move, teams regroup and new branches emerge.
