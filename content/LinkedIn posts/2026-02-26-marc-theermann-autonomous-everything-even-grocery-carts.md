## Full text

Autonomous everything. Even grocery carts.

This might be my favorite robot of the day.

In China, autonomous shopping carts are already being tested that follow customers automatically, avoid obstacles, and navigate crowded markets on their own.

From a systems point of view, it’s simple but powerful robotics: local navigation, human-following, collision avoidance, and reliable indoor autonomy — all applied to a problem everyone understands.

If this scales, grocery stores change quietly but meaningfully:
no more abandoned carts in parking lots, no more employees pushing dozens at once, and smoother store operations overall.
