Why do people help delivery robots?

Here in Helsinki, small delivery robots move around the city. Twice now, I’ve seen one get stuck on a curb, and people stopped their walk or bike ride to help it continue.

What feels strange to me isn’t only that the robot doesn’t feel stress or gratitude. It’s also that helping it feels satisfying, even though it’s just a machine.

Around the same spot, I once saw a couple arguing at a bus stop. Something was clearly wrong, but no one stepped in. Not because it felt dangerous, but because it wasn't clear if help was wanted or what helping would even mean.

With the robot, there's no ambiguity. You see the problem, you know the solution, you help. No risk of overstepping, no need to read the room. Maybe we don't help robots because they're cute. We help them because it's simple. The need is obvious, the action is clear, and there's no social risk.

I wonder how much help we miss giving each other, not from lack of care, but from lack of clarity.
