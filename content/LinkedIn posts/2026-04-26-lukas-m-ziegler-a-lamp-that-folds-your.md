A lamp that folds your clothes! 🛋️

Syncere unveiled Lume, a robotic home assistant disguised as a ceiling lamp that does chores unobtrusively.

For years, debate raged: should robot butlers be humanoid or substantially different? Lume offers a third way, the assistive robot as essentially disguised furniture. 

The idea is that helpers should work on the sly, unobtrusive, where humanoid designs would be too conspicuous.

The "scoop" light shields of Lume turn into helping hands. The robot quietly moves from the head of the bed to the foot and starts folding clothes. When you don't need chores done, Lume provides light.

The technology uses imitation and reinforcement learning to teach folding based on human behavior. Safety is embedded through compliant motor controls, 360-degree awareness, fabric on joints to avoid pinch points, and mechanical shutters that conceal sensors when not in use.

Founder originally got the idea from Disney's Beauty and the Beast, Lumiere, the talking candelabra.

Instead of a conspicuous humanoid standing in your room, your furniture does chores.
