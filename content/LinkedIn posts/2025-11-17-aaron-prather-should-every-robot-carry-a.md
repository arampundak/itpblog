## Full text

As robots move out of industrial settings and onto our sidewalks, airports, malls, and other public locations, one question is becoming impossible to ignore:

Should every robot be required to carry a "black box" - just like airplanes?

But with that comes some questions and issues to solve, such as:

- Why accountability and transparency may push regulators to mandate robot black boxes

- The privacy, security, and cost concerns that make this far more complicated than it seems

- How insurers, not governments, may ultimately force the issue

- Why the robotics industry must engage now - before the rules are written for us

Public-facing robots are scaling fast. And history shows that when incidents happen, policymakers don't negotiate... they regulate.

So what happens when robots must record everything?
And are we ready for what that means?
