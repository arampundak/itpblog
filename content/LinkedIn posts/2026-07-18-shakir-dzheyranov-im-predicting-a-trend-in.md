I'm predicting a trend in Robotics. Have you heard of the term "Warm Tech."?

For me, it's when technology doesn't try to look like cold sci-fi. Think of Warm Tech as the design layer that makes a robot and its brand feel legible, safe, and socially acceptable around humans. Not by making it cute. By reducing perceived threat.

Here are usual ingredients that form this look in design:

- Warm colors. Less sterile, less techy.
- Softer, slower animation and robot movement. No jittery, glitchy, digital effects.
- More human fonts and Less "Terminal" looking fonts. And, god forbid, no ASCII effect.
- Softer materials, with slight textures.
- Friendly robot naming. Instead of "FLD#14L".

Especially in robotics, this feels like a trend now and we'll see more examples. I think 1X def tried to own this visual branding category. But today I want you to look at Isaac I by Weave Robotics. Isaac and it's brand is a great example of Warm Tech. My respect to co-founders Kaan Dogrusoz and Evan Wineland. By the way, pre-orders open.
