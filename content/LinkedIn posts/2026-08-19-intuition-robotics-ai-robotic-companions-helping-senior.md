## Full text

CBS News New York recently highlighted how the New York State Office for the Aging is working with ElliQ to bring proactive, supportive technology into the homes of older adults, helping people age with greater connection, independence, and dignity.

For Georgeann Krell, 78, ElliQ has become part of everyday life after her husband passed. ElliQ encourages her throughout the day, plays cognitive games with her, helps her stay on top of her schedule, and prompts her to stay active and engaged. "It's been a really difficult time, but she even asks me if I need a hug."

Stories like Georgeann's reflect what this partnership is really about. As Greg Olsen said, "What we're trying to do is to complement what we already do."

We believe technology for aging should never replace human connection, it should strengthen it. ElliQ is designed to do exactly that: to show up in the quiet moments, to encourage activity and routine, to spark conversation, and to help older adults feel seen, supported, and less alone between visits from family, friends, and caregivers.
