## Full text

Just saw this mind-blowing video breaking down the internals of Disney’s Olaf robot (those shoulder joints are wild engineering!). I’m not usually into Disney movies lately, but I’ll make an exception for Olaf and for what NVIDIA’s team pulled off.

Everyone’s talking about the chips and breakthroughs, but tech doesn’t have to be all serious. 
The Olaf robot at GTC combines NVIDIA’s AI, robotics, and real-time simulation tech like motion planning, computer vision, and speech-to-animation pipelines all running on their GPUs.

It’s a fun, tangible example of how high-performance computing can power playful applications. It frankly makes me wish my old math and physics teachers had brought the same kind of inspiration into the classroom. 
Who knows how many future scientists that could have sparked.
