How to create robots that are not only useful, but also elegant?

A robot's character doesn’t stem solely from its appearance — it’s also in the timing and style of how it moves. Robot motion should go beyond fulfilling functional goals and constraints , incorporating trajectories that communicate intention, attention, convey emotions and personality.

In our recent paper, ELEGNT, we introduce a robotic lamp inspired by Pixar’s Luxo Jr. to explore how expressive versus functional-driven movements shape human-robot interactions .
