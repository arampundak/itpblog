## Full text

Had a green that just wouldn't settle



On screen it looked great. In hand, another story..

Too clean next to white.

Too soft against black.

And on a slightly textured plastic, it started to feel a bit cheap.

Nothing dramatic. Just not sitting where it should.

We ended up nudging it more than expected.

Pulled some of the yellow out. Added a touch of grey to give it weight.
Changed the sheen so it didn’t catch the light in a harsh way.

At some point it stopped being about the colour and became about everything around it...can be the way with colour..

That’s usually the way with green.

It’s not passive. It pushes and pulls on the rest of the product.

Get it right and it carries a lot of the work.

Get it wrong and it exposes everything - muddy, soulless..

Green needs a bit of restraint, and it always needs to be seen in context.

It’s one of the few colours that makes you design the whole product, not just the surface.

And yes, it is a celebration of St Paddy's Day!!
