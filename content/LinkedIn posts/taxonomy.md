# Taxonomy

Fixed vocabulary. Posts take 1-3 tags. Do not invent new tags without adding them here first.

## `humanoid-hardware`
actuators, DOF, manipulation, bipeds, form factor, materials, mechanical design

## `design-practice`
industrial design process, CMF, rendering, prototyping, craft, portfolio, design critique

## `hri-legibility`
expressiveness, social cues, trust, anthropomorphism, companions, how robots are read by people

## `character-robots`
entertainment, Disney, animatronics, performance, character design for robots

## `physical-ai`
world models, VLA, reinforcement learning, sim2real, embodied training, foundation models

## `policy-safety`
regulation, liability, standards, certification, alignment, public deployment rules

## `market-funding`
raises, valuations, stealth launches, acquisitions, unit economics, industry structure

## `ai-tools`
agents, LLM workflows, vibe coding, AI-assisted making

## Fields that are not tags

- `career_relevant: true` — hiring, roles, opportunities. Orthogonal to subject.
- `entities: []` — companies, labs, products named in the post (Fauna, Disney Research, 1X ...).
