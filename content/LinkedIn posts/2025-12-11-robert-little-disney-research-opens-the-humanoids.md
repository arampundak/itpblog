Disney Research opens the Humanoids Summit with a combination of engineering and entertainment

The Humanoids Summit kicked off with Disney Research showing something only Disney can pull off: serious humanoid engineering wrapped in pure character performance.

We were treated to a live look at the new humanoid Olaf, and Moritz Baecher walked us through how they made it possible. He explained the engineering behind the magic:
✨ advanced simulation to design motion that feels alive
🔥 thermal optimization to keep actuators compact and safe
🤖 model-based control to achieve expressive, repeatable character movements
⚙️ careful actuator design to balance force, speed, and personality

It’s impressive how much engineering goes into making a robot endearing.

One can argue that humanoids are not ready for prime time. But Disney shows that these characters are ready today. Olaf is a humanoid I can get behind.

ATI Industrial Automation and Celera Motion, A Novanta Company are sponsoring the Humanoids Summit and continue to support physical AI.
