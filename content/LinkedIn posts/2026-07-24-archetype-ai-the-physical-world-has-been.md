## Full text

The physical world has been producing data for years. Almost none of it gets understood.

Before founding Archetype AI, our CEO and co-founder, Ivan Poupyrev, PhD led Project Soli at Google: a radar sensor small enough to fit on a chip, precise enough to track sub-millimeter motion, cheap enough to build into everyday objects.

Soli proved something important. The physical world can be captured as real-time data, at scale, from sensors that live anywhere.

What was missing was a model that could make sense of what the data meant.

That's Newton -> A world model that learns physics directly from raw sensor data, so machines can perceive, reason, and act on the world they're already measuring.

Physical AI has been Ivan's lifelong ambition. Newton is where it becomes infrastructure.
