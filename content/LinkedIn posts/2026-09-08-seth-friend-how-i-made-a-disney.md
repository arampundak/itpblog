## Full text

It stands!

The humanoid robot Christopher Medellin-Martinez and I have been building now stands in real life and walks in simulation. I just posted a video sharing the build and our progress so far, check it out!

Video: https://lnkd.in/gr4F-spG

Our prototype weighs 7 kg, has 20 degrees of freedom, and costs roughly $1,700 in parts.

The goal is to build an affordable humanoid platform that gives students and researchers hands-on experience with mechanics, electronics, controls, and robot learning.

Next up: transferring walking from NVIDIA's Isaac Sim to the physical robot and measuring how reliably it performs.

Thank you to Texas Inventionworks and Scott Evans for the workspace, guidance, and support along the way.
