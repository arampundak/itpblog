## Full text

Watch people near a humanoid robot and you see the same thing every time. There's fascination, but also a quiet "I'll keep my distance."

The hardware is brilliant. The behavior is what's missing. When a robot turns toward you, it should tell you why. Is it curious? Does it need help? Is it watching you? Right now you can't read them, and we don't trust what we can't read. That's a design problem as much as an engineering one.

This week I spent some time thinking about what it'll take to make these machines likable enough that we actually accept them into our lives. A lot of it comes down to behaving in a way we instantly understand. And no, they don't all have to look like black-and-white Stormtroopers.
