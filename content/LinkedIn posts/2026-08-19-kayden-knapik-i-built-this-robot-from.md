## Full text

I built this robot from scratch in under 1 week.

Meet Stompy, a 6-DOF bipedal robot that I built in my living room in less than a week, including the complete CAD design, 3D printing, reinforcement learning training, and sim-to-real deployment.

Stompy is fully 3D printed and runs on:
- NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano
- 6x Robstride O2 Quasi-Direct Drive actuators
- MPU9250 IMU

And the entire robot costs around $1,000.

The design was inspired by the Walker robot from Fauna Robotics.

Stompy will be 100% open source, and I'll be sharing a full YouTube video soon covering the entire build process.

I strongly believe that one of the best ways to learn robotics is to build it yourself.

A few years ago, building a capable bipedal robot at home would have been extremely difficult and expensive. Today, for around $1,000, you can build your own robot, train it to walk in simulation, and deploy that policy directly onto the real robot.

And this is only the beginning.

Let me know what you think of Stompy!
