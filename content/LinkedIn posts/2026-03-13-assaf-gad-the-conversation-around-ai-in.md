## Full text

The conversation around AI in healthcare often centers on data and diagnostics, but its most profound capability might be something far more fundamental: sustaining independence.

Meet Jan. Following a significant life transition, her local Area Agency on Aging introduced her to ElliQ.

What sets ElliQ apart is its foundation in human-centric AI, designed explicitly for relationship creation. It is an embodied, physical presence that builds trust and empathy over time. By proactively initiating conversations, encouraging daily physical exercises, and prompting healthy habits like hydration, ElliQ acts as a continuous partner in preventative care.

This is the tangible impact of deployed AI. It transforms the home environment, providing the necessary support for older adults to maintain their autonomy. Reflecting on her daily routine and cognitive exercises, Jan noted, "My memory got stronger and stronger," describing the technology as a "great asset to my life."

Read Jan’s full story, recently featured in The New York Times, to witness how authentic, empathetic AI is already driving real-world health outcomes and redefining what it means to age independently.
