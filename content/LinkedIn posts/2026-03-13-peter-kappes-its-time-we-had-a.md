## Full text

It's time we had a conversation about this.

Watch the humanoid robot space long enough and you see three approaches in the face repeating. The ones that feel safe will win the homes.

I've named these three visual approaches.
The Snugglies. The Sentries & The Voidies.

So many companies are rushing to put any design into homes, 
assuming the masses will embrace whatever they build. 

Some of these designs don't exactly scream 
"I'm your friend and want to help."

Have you done the studies? Actually done them?

Because this is what you're up against. 
Decades of Hollywood. 
Billions of dollars of programming telling us to side-eye humanoid robots. 

Has any company ever rebranded out of a category consumers already decided?

McDonald's is fast food, & will always be considered fast food, 
because of cultural conditioning.
If you add some truffle fries & call it gourmet, nobody's taking a first date there.

In 1985, Coca-Cola replaced its 99-year-old formula with "New Coke,"
Sales plummeted and the original returned in just 79 days.

HBO Max dropped "HBO" for "Max" & spent years walking it back. 

Cracker Barrel modernized their logo. 
How did that go?

Try casting Luke Skywalker as a villain. 
No matter what he does, you'll still be waiting for him to use the Force.

You cannot rebrand decades of cultural conditioning overnight.

The Voidies are basically the result of "yes" men nodding in unison while decades of Hollywood foreshadowing plays on mute in the background.

If you're building for the factory, awesome. Function wins.

But if you're designing for the home, show me the market studies. 
Not the ones tracking what influencers think is cool. 
I'm talking about the billions of people who watched the same movies you did.

If you want a home humanoid, it has to be inviting, approachable and warm. 

Voidies at 3AM while you're sleepy in your underwear? 
Those silhouettes don't exactly spark joy.
Snugglies? You grab your water, mumble hey buddy, & go back to sleep.

Here is how I see this playing out.
The Snugglies have the higher chance of winning in the home. 
They're approachable, huggable, & they look like they want to help.

The Sentries are the in-betweens. They look like Power Rangers or Halo characters. Protectors of society. You trust them to guard, not to tuck you in. 
They'll find their way into some homes & factories.

The Voidies can own the line. They can dominate commercial spaces. 
But the hallway at night? This aesthetic does not invite trust.

Yes, a digital face can soften the look. But if you think the masses embrace this dark aesthetic after decades of Hollywood conditioning, we'll just have to let time tell.

If you want the home, build Snugglies.
If you want both, build Sentries.
If you want the factory, build Voidies.


Which would you prefer in your homes?
Snugglies, Sentries, or Voidies?
