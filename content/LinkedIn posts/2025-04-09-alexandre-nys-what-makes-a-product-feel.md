## Full text

What makes a product feel modern?

We all know: it’s about what’s removed, not what’s added...
But if we look closer: it’s also about how it’s refined.

A topic which isn’t taught enough (at all?) in design schools (especially in Western ones), yet it’s a crucial aspect of our job.

I often ask younger designers when they show me their work:
“What have you brought as a designer that an engineer wouldn’t?”

--

When it comes to cosmetics, the most important value a designer can bring is their sensitivity to form:

👉 Surface treatment
👉 Part line positioning
👉 Transitions between materials

They’re the choices that turn a product into a desirable object, not just a functional one. 

The difference between a product that’s tolerated… And one that people perceive as modern, sexy, and fall in love with. This is one of the key ways design adds value to a company.
