 Disney AI Brings Robots to Life!

 The Innovation
• Disney + ETH Zurich scientists an AI system that generates realistic, physics-based movements for virtual characters and robots
• Works from simple text or image inputs

 How It Works
1. Two-stage approach:
 • Learns motion representation from a large dataset
 • Trains control policy via reinforcement learning
2. Handles diverse motions:
 • From simple walking to complex acrobatics
 • Outperforms previous methods in accuracy and adaptability

 Real-World Application
• Adapts to physical constraints
• Transfers motions to real robots
• Maintains balance and style

 Proof of Concept
• Robot dances and stays upright when pushed (built using low-cost hardware)

 Why It Matters
• Bridges the gap between animation and robotics
• Helps humanoids move naturally and adapt to new situations
• Personal robots expected by 2025?
