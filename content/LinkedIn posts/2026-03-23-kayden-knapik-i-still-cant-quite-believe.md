I still can’t quite believe this…

We just won ETH Robotics Club’s HACK2026 🏆

Going into this, I honestly didn’t even expect to get accepted, and 48 hours later we are standing on stage as winners.

And yeah, I may have teared up a bit in front of 200+ people 😅

In that time, we built a humanoid robot system aimed at helping address one of Europe’s biggest challenges, labour shortages. We designed it with applications like restaurants in mind, for example delivering items like pizza. 

It can autonomously navigate outside using GPS and compass data, plan waypoints from typed instructions, and walk to its destination. It can also autonomously pick up and drop objects, for example handling a Coke can and placing it into a bag.

We combined imitation learning from Hugging Face’s LeRobot and used NVIDIA Robotics GR00T Whole Body Control to bring everything together.

I could not be more proud of what we pulled off as a team. Huge shoutout to Cezar Solovastru, Miguel Huber, Benjamin Knöbel del Olmo, Xinran Wang and Natcha Jengjirapas. 

Also a big thank you to Martino Russi from Hugging Face for the help with LeRobot and imitation learning. That made a massive difference for us.

Huge thanks to the ETH Robotics Club and Declan Shine for organizing such an incredible event and bringing together such a strong group of people.

And it was amazing to see support from partners like OpenAI , Hugging Face , Virtuals Protocol,Rerun , and General Catalyst .

More soon. This one was special.
