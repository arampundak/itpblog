## Full text

Honda is returning to robotics, but not with another humanoid.

Instead, the automaker is betting the future of factory automation lies in one of robotics' hardest problems: dexterous manipulation. Its new multi-fingered robotic hand can grasp tiny screws, assemble connectors, and perform delicate tasks that have long challenged industrial robots.

The technology marks a strategic shift from ASIMO's public-facing legacy toward practical manufacturing applications powered by Physical AI.

As labor shortages grow and factories demand greater flexibility, Honda's biggest robotics breakthrough may not be a robot that walks, but a hand that works.
