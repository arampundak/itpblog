Last year, J.A.B.O.C (Just A Band Of Creatives) celebrated the beauty of metal during Singapore Design Week. 

This year, under the curatorial baton of Edmund Zhang and stewardship of Winnie Lim, J.A.B.O.C presents Playful Living and invites people to fundamentally rethink play and how it can be applied in creative, unconventional ways.

Alongside other talented creatives, Roger Ng Wei Lun, Edwind Tan, @maggie, Li Xue L., Hsiao Fong Lee, Shawn Ng, Esther N. and this year’s partner in crime, You Shan Ang, we are proud to unveil the fruits of our labour.

Keen to find out more? Drop by Golden Seed at Millenia Walk from 11 to 21 September, 11am to 7pm.

Live life and come play a little. We hope to see you then!
