## Full text

This will be the real essence, value & cuteness of #robots in our lives. Join me on a journey to inspire a positive image of robots as non-threatening and helpful to humans. We should be thinking about robots not as our replacement but more creatively, as our partners (or tools) in what we are trying to achieve, like our smartphones or laptops.

This is such a mesmerising human-camera interaction! It's not just cool, but carries a profound meaning too. The interaction between AI, cameras, and smartphones has heaps of potential for the future. For instance, imagine a camera or smartphone AI recognizing, analyzing, and providing information to enhance user experiences.

Picture this, you're using your camera to snap a photo of a building. The AI could automatically recognise the building and display relevant info on the screen. Maybe a brief history about the architecture pops up or links to further details.

Or think of an AI during a video call via your smartphone camera analyzing facial expressions. It could recognize emotions and help the user better connect by giving hints about moods or reactions. The integration of AI in cameras and smartphones opens up heaps of possibilities for personalized, helpful, and more informative experiences. It's exciting to see how these technologies could evolve!
