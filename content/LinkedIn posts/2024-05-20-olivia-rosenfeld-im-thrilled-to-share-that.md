## Gist

Celebrates winning a global Sanofi medical award for a Bezalel design collaboration that improves doctor–pediatric-patient dialogue.

## Full text

I'm thrilled to share that together with my medical team at Sanofi, we won 1st place in the Global medical award- “act for patients” category, out of 171 submissions from 27 countries!
The success of this winning project was made possible because we stepped out of our comfort zone and partnered with the Bezalel Academy of Art and Design.
This outstanding collaboration brought the creative world of art into the sphere of patient’s care, and created an innovative solution that facilitates meaningful dialogue between pediatric patients and their physicians, designed to improve disease burden conversations and ultimately their quality of care.
I'd like to specifically acknowledge our Medical Manger Inga Antonyan Elon for her creativity and leadership, and the dedicated team members: Paz Ordan, Tujan Irshied, Ines Tzach-Schleifer, Pazit Waisemberger, and Dafna Noyman, with special thanks to our Bezalel Academy of Art and Design partners led by Safi hefetz and the team Ron Livay Aram Pundak Yasmine Szendro Brown and of course Tina Meerry Melusyan and Noa Geffen, M.D for their continuous support .
This project could not have been done without your professionalism and dedication.
