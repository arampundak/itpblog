## Full text

News article (in Hebrew) by Hadas Erel and me about our 10-years long research at Reichman University in social robotics research and how non-humanoid robotics gestures are interpreted automatically as social cues.

This research direction was pioneered many years ago by Cynthia Breazeal Guy Hoffman Leila Takayama Wendy Ju and others in the #HRI community.

The recent research by Apple's on a new expressive robotic lamp brought the topic to light.
