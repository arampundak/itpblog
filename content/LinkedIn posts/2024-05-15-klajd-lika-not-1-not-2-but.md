## Gist

## Full text

Not 1, not 2 but more than 20 Humanoids in the making... some ready to buy for USD 16k!

- It is worth mentioning the incredible job done by Unitree Robotics to bring in the market the G1 Humanoid. A small robot, human teenager size, is out there and can perform some very nice moves. A platform for research, ready to be used to accomplish tasks. We are very much looking forward to see which tasks they are going to automate and help us in everyday life.

- AI enthusiasts have been waiting for this moment to "place some intelligence" on these machines. I must say that... there is no excuse anymore! We have the technological pieces ready to assemble.
Superhuman actuation, Superhuman vision, Superhuman haptic feedback, Superhuman computational power!

- Robots for services in healthcare will be revolutionizing the industry by covering all vacancies in hospitals and collaborating with nurses and doctors. Miroki, the adorable robot from Enchanted Tools designed specifically for this purpose.

At Bota Systems AG we are there to support and provide the necessary technology for robust and reliable force feedback.

On a side note:
5 years ago in Montreal, Canada we watched a similar show by 4-legged robots startups showing off their incredible "RoboDogs". They were performing outstanding physical capabilities. Therefore, I thought that I couldn't miss the opportunity to be in IEEE RAS ICRA this year and see the same happening with Humanoids. These events are extremely fascinating and never fail to impress us.
Although, 4-legged robots still haven't replaced jobs done by our beloved animal friends (dogs) and the physical capabilities are there since more than 5 years... Having a robot doing backflips, running, jumping and outperforming humans or other animals in terms of physical capabilities doesn't replace us, but has the potential to empower us. Robots are alike all other tools and machines.
A human with an axe can cut a tree for wood to build a shelter. A human without an axe, stays in the cave. :) We should endorse technology and use it wisely for good.
