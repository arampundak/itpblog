## Full text

Something I wanted to make since day 1 of image models is to be able to turn my ugly renders into something magnificent.
It's not really a secret that some image models are more aesthetic and realistic than others. 

Some produce nicer images (Flux Krea or Wan), others would follow the prompts really well while looking like plastic (Qwen), and some are just really good at editing.

Here, I'm using Flux to generate scenes, and then comes the trick.

1. Take your ugly render and your pretty scene to an editing model (Nano Banana and Qwen Edit are my top 2)
2. But instead of telling it to redo the render in a reference style, ask it to replace the subject in your reference. Gemini models can also help to make a detailed prompt for the task as Qwen, for example, requires overprompting.

Still works quite wonky - Nano Banana can fail >90% of the time on some prompts, Qwen is less advanced, so it requires a lot of tweaking by default, but its power is ComfyUI and everything that you can do with it. 
Both are still far more advanced than what you could do with controlnets, relighting, IP adapters, or anything else. 
And the craziest thing - it didn't even exist half a year ago.

Obviously, once done, apart from the images themselves, you've made some proper backplate images, as well as nice lighting reference, in case you ever wanna go into 3D. 

But on that - in another post.
