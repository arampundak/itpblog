## Full text

One of the biggest secrets to loving what you do? Surrounding yourself with brilliant, inspiring, slightly mad people. 

They say you are the average of the people you spend time with, which explains why I have had a modicum of success in my career. I’ve had the good fortune of knowing and working alongside some incredible people. 

On this stereotypically dull Monday commute, I felt compelled to share some incredible people from around me that have inspired me in one way or another recently. Perhaps they will do the same for you: 

Jamie Clements - A breathwork expert who has built a safe space for discussions around mens health, a successful podcast speaking to heroes from my childhood like Jonny Wilkinson, as well as his own App. Amazing work mate, cant wait to see what you do next.

Sebastian Clark - Head of Creative at Huel, a brand doing amazing things in the nutrition space. Incredibly talented creative but recently he shared a post on how he deliberately turned up late to an important meeting because he promised his son he would say goodbye in the morning. As a father myself, this was a beautiful reminder of whats actually important. 

Shaun Wellens- Head of Design at Apptronik, a company building humanoid robots for the real world. Since I met Shaun, his work ethic has been unparalleled. His renders and conceptual provocations always inspire me to explore more, think bigger and play more often. On top of that he's a class bloke. 

Lasse Roxrud Farstad & Øystein Helle Husby - Who have created the most inspiring micro-brand in horology for years. Straum designs beautiful watches inspired by Norwegian nature. These two work harder than anyone I know. Their attention to detail is second to none. If you haven't seen their watches yet, they just had a new limited edition launch in Titanium with the fantastic Fratello. So so proud and excited to see them develop this brand further. 

Didrik Dege Dimmen - CEO Auk. Just sold their 100,000th unit! The fact that my friends back home know AUK is a testament to the power of this product. Watching them go from success to success, is amazing. Proof of his and his teams work ethic, especially given how hard it is as a startup in Norway. Congrats on the big news mate. Next up, 500k.

My colleagues and teams in reMarkable - you know who you are. These guys inspire me every day. The amount of fun we have at work and the amount of work we get done is nothing short of incredible. You guys inspire me daily to be a better leader and make me excited about where we are headed as a company. 
There are so many more, but i'll hit the word limit. Some others I have to mention: Joe Slatter founder of Design Burger. Emma Walder running global events for big names. Liam Man a light painter and photographer and Joe Gillibrand doing bits at New Balance. 

Here’s to surrounding ourselves with people who lift us higher, challenge our thinking, broaden our horizons and keep the journey fun.

Have a great Monday ☕
