Why am I optimistic about the future of #robotics? Here is why


Introducing children to robotics early can foster a sense of curiosity, creativity, and comfort with technology that shapes their perception of robots in a positive light. Hands-on experiences with robots, whether through educational kits, coding programs, or interactive demonstrations, demystify the technology and highlight its potential to solve real-world problems. This early exposure helps children view robots as tools for collaboration rather than competition, reducing the fear of being replaced or threatened.

Every robotics conference should have outreach programs where young people are brought in to see the tech and see what is possible. That is how we build the future of robots and people working together for the common good.
