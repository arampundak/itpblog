## Full text

We are excited that our paper, “ReActor: Reinforcement Learning for Physics-Aware Motion Retargeting”, has been accepted to SIGGRAPH 2026.

We present an RL–based retargeting method that transfers human motion to diverse morphologies, including humanoids and a quadruped, while avoiding common artifacts such as foot sliding, hovering, and self-penetration. Our bilevel optimization framework jointly refines upper-level retargeting parameters, defined through sparse semantic correspondences, while training the lower-level RL policy, eliminating the need for manual correspondence tuning.
