## Full text

The market continues to segment everything into vertical robotics and humanoids as this report does. There's a huge blank canvas in the middle for human-capable robotics that are more trustworthy, more capable (e.g. move larger loads faster), more friendly, more cost-effective, and available now.

As one of our investors pointed out recently, to make a truly great return, you have to find those opportunities that are both contrarian and right. Glad that Sequoia Capital and General Catalyst among others (Neo, Khosla Ventures, Mayo Clinic, Lux Capital, Bison Ventures, MVP Ventures, Industry Ventures) have made that bet to the tune of nearly $150M raised on us.

Cobots are the future. I predict next year we'll begin to see humanoid companies come to grips with the practical challenges of cost and control of an over-actuated system and begin to simplify into the non-humanoid space. Already we're seeing a move toward wheeled mobility rather than bipedalism.
