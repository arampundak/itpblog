What are the average values of the main metrics in humanoid robot design?
ChatGPT doesn’t give you these numbers directly, so take note! 

While working with humanoid robot companies especially in the early stages, I’ve noticed that teams often get confused about the most important metrics.

I thought this could be a good opportunity to analyze 46 recent humanoid robots and extract some average numbers.

It’s important to mention that decisions about these metrics should always be based on applications and context, but sometimes it’s also helpful to look at the market for reference.

Average humanoid metrics: 

Height: 164.5 cm / ≈ 5’5”
Walking Speed: 4.44 km/h / ≈ 2.76 mph
Weight: 65.9 kg / ≈ 145.3 lb
Payload: 12.3 kg / ≈ 27.1 lb
DoF (Degrees of Freedom): 30.7
Battery Runtime: 2.4 hours / 144 min

Some extreme cases were removed to avoid skewing the data.
