What’s the deal with Amazon buying Fauna Robotics?

On one hand: great robot, strong team.

On the other: humanoids are nowhere near ready for the home. Safety, reliability, and real usefulness are still a long way off.

Amazon has tested Digit for warehouse tote movement and reportedly explored its own humanoid efforts internally.

Amazon's FAR lab has also done impressive work with humanoids from Chinese company Unitree Robotics. Unitree has built a solid business selling its humanoids for R&D purposes. Maybe Fauna becomes a U.S.-based alternative for researchers?

Amazon had a tough time finding viable use cases for its Astro home robot -- a much simpler, wheeled robot. It can't think things will go better for a humanoid that costs $50K? Can it?

Genuinely curious how others are thinking about this one.
