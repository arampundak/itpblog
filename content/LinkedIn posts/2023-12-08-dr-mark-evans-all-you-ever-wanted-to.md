All you ever wanted to know about the doctorate in design but were afraid to ask.......

Having managed to squeeze in one final European Union funded Erasmus+ project before Brexit, D.Doc provides an overview of doctoral education in design for Erasmus+ member countries using data collected during 2022/23:

· D.Doc Open Educational Resource (OER): https://d-doc.eu/
· Launch Webinar: https://lnkd.in/e-VaVjvs

A collaboration between four universities (Loughborough, Sapienza, Aalto, METU) and Belgian educational technology company (ATiT), the project maps the nature of the European doctorate in design using information gathered from institutional webpages; requests for confirmation of data; case studies from holders of completed doctorates; interviews with researchers; and material provided by design schools/departments. The D.Doc open educational resource (OER) provides the following:

· Interactive map indicating location and intensity of doctoral activity
· Fourteen searchable characteristics e.g. educational model, subject specialities, duration
· A-Z list of institutions offering doctorates in design
· 20 Impact Case Studies and 14 Research through Design (RtD) Case Studies as PDF downloads with title/institution search
· 15 reflective video interviews with researchers of completed doctorates answering questions such as motivation, choice of institution and advice for future candidates
· Searchable titles of 943 on-going doctorates with start date and institution of study

As with any academic research, there were limitations to the study that included the problematic nature of information openly available at the time of collection, responsiveness of institutions to confirm/provide information and capacity of researchers to contribute case study material.
