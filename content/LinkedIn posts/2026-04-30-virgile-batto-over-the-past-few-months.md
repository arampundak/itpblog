## Full text

Over the past few months, I’ve been working on an open-source humanoid as part of the Hugging Face LeRobot team.
For now, it’s a fully 3D-printed biped designed for fast iteration, low cost, and real-world experimentation.
Here’s a first behavior: stable real-time balance, with continuous micro-adjustments driven by an RL policy.
Under $2,500 to build, and a full leg can be reprinted and repaired in a day.
The goal is simple: make humanoid robotics easier to build, test, and improve.
Still early, but already a useful platform to iterate on hardware, control, and sim-to-real.
I’ll be presenting and open-sourcing this work at GoSim Paris on May 6, in the Open Source Robotics session.
