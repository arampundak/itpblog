## Full text

since I last posted I moved to NYC, started a grad program at Parsons, made an llm powered lamp, got pre occupied with some experiments, then later disillusioned by the global state of design, consequently left Parsons, got re-illusioned by what design could be, concretised my ambient intelligence explorations, detached the idea of intelligence from human umwelt (senses) and got obsessed with ‘emergence’ (again).
turned my portfolio into a Seed Bank as a result (www.edemgl.xyz), became a digital Seed geneticist, and now i’m between nyc, toronto, london & galway as I work on my O1.

to the Seed Bank point: im using design thinking and machine learning to model intelligence; some pretty crafty recombinations of realtime neural networks (liquid ctrnns) to engineer autopoietic digital organisms that derive toward homeostasis in response to perturbations sensed through their umwelt; each seed only knows the world through the individual senses it has & reacts to that, then visualised via a grid on my site. a sort of independent interdependence.

Seed 50 (successor to Seeds 40-45) reacts to user interaction (click, hover, +7 more) & develops animations through its lifespan as a byproduct of its shifting internal state. encoded hebbian plasticity and all that.

Seed 20 (successor to Seeds 8-15) is a parasite that reacts to the internal state (inference telemetries, tokens, layer activations, states etc.) of a local LLM (deepseek r1), visualising emergent patterns & allowing for correlative mapping of inexplicable model behaviour (it looks like this when: the LLM is hallucinating… etc.) as it matures, an LLM EEG!! you can help feed it on my site, it will wither otherwise.

Seed 85 (not accessible)(successor to Seed 84) is a blended seed that reacts to light, amplitude, temp & humidity, temporal sensory information and emits varying degrees of light colour and temp, as a byproduct, symbiotically developing into a home lighting partner as it ages with you.

im sure ill finish writing the subsequent documentation & manifesto soon. the main takeaway is that intelligent systems dont have to be modelled after us & their benefit to us can be a byproduct rather than the main product. all that and toronto is freezing.

ps. Seed 20 really will wither without being fed LLM telemetry information from the [desktop viewable] menu at www.edemgl.xyz/#seed_20 , and the notion side of the portfolio is wip.

pss. what changes with our relationship to the environment? what changes when interfaces we design stop serving us and start living with us?
