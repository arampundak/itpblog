# Don't Confuse Universal With Boring

## Why I saved it

## Gist

Argues that "normal"-looking products are the result of a sophisticated design process that imitators consistently fail to capture.

## Full text

There's a fine line between designing something standard and creating something universal.

The pursuit of minimal style and low cost has led to a flood of boring, repetitive products aimed at being "normal," but they often just follow trends and compete on price.

I've been reflecting on how products that seem "normal" are actually the result of a very sophisticated process, and those who try to imitate often fall short.

Capturing the essence of simplicity is anything but simple.
