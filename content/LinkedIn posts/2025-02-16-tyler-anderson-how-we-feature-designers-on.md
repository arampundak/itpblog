# How We Feature Designers On Render Weekly

## Gist

Introduces Render Weekly's new website as a virtual design studio for sharing, critiquing, and refining industrial-design work.

## Full text

Industrial design has always thrived in studio environments where work gets pinned up, critiqued, and refined through iteration. As more of the design process moves online, that kind of structured, collaborative space has become harder to find.

That essence is what we are trying to capture with Render Weekly's new website. It is a virtual design studio where designers can share their work, develop a body of projects, and engage in a space that functions more like a real design practice than just a social feed.

In our recent blog post, we broke down how we feature designers on Render Weekly and why the virtual studio approach matters. This is not just about showcasing the best renders. It is about creating a space where designers can continuously refine their craft, get feedback, and gain visibility in a meaningful way.

If you are looking for a space to challenge yourself, share your work, and build your portfolio, the platform is open.
