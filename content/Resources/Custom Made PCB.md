Terminology:

Terminal Block Footprint**
- Add a 2-pin terminal block to your PCB
- Solar panel wires screw into the terminal block
- Footprint = standard terminal block (2.54mm or 5.08mm spacing)

Solder Pads**

- Create two large solder pads on your PCB
- Solder the solar panel wires directly to these pads
- Footprint = two rectangular copper pads

CONN_2 - Header Pins**

- Solder header pins to your PCB
- Crimp connectors on solar panel wires
- Footprint = 1x2 header (2.54mm spacing)
