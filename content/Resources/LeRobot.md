**LeRobot** is an open-source robotics learning framework by Hugging Face designed to make robot learning as accessible as working with ML datasets and models on Hugging Face.
It:

- Connects to the arm over USB
- Sends joint commands
- Reads encoder positions
- Records trajectorie
- Replays motions
- Trains policies
