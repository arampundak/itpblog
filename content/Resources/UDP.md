Universal Datagram Protocol

Packet Connections
UDP sends individual packets with no guarantee they arrive, arrive in order, or arrive at all.
“Throwing messages into the air and hoping they land”
UDP is used when speed matters more than accuracy, losing a packet is acceptable or latency must be minimal. I is used for live video, games or audio streamings.
Its fast, unordered and lightweight. It doesn't know if anyone is listening, if the messege arrived or if the receiver exists.
