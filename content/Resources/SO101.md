The SO-ARM100 is ==an open-source, 6-degree-of-freedom, 3D-printed robotic arm designed for AI, imitation learning, and education==, often paired with the Hugging Face LeRobot library. It is a low-cost, DIY-friendly, 6-axis arm, often used in pairs to teach robots tasks through teleoperation.

It includes:
- 6 smart servo motors
- Mechanical links
- Controller board
- USB connection
- Gripper
