- **Repository (repo):** The folder for your project, once Git is tracking it. It contains all your files plus Git's record of every change you've made.
- **Commit:** A saved snapshot of your project at a specific moment in time. Think of it as a milestone you can always return to.
- **Staging:** The step where you tell Git _which_ files to include in your next commit. More on this in [Section 5](https://github.com/ellennickles/code-your-way-s26/blob/main/version-control-guides/git.md#5-add-and-commit-changes).
- **Branch:** A parallel version of your project. You can experiment on a branch without affecting your main work. More on this in [Section 7](https://github.com/ellennickles/code-your-way-s26/blob/main/version-control-guides/git.md#7-branching-and-merging).
