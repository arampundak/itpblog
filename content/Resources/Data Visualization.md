**Tables**
Tables are good for precise information

**What are the components of Data Viz**
Purpose - why you do it? what information I want to communicate, why am I doing it?
this set a goal and a scope:
Content - make a point, serve a purpose. Limit what belongs and what doesn't - its tempting to put more then needed
Cite your sources, make the knowledge transparent.
Story / Concept - most important are top left, big titles, think how you layout the information
Formatting / Metaphor / Visual Form - Shapes, Colors
Think of your audience

**Visualization serve different purpose**
Purpose - Explanatory != Exploratory
Explanatory: question - look - answer
Exploratory: look - ask - look - answer (no particular question in the data vis)
Most things have both

**Why Visualize?**
Find patterns, Sample dataset, Summarizing data, give a visual idea of the data!

In tables: Use white space intelligently.
In bar chart: have horizontal lines.
