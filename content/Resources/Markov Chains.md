A [Markov chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_chain) is a mathematical system that moves from one state to another based on fixed probability rules, where the next step depends only on the current state and not on the past.
Markov chains rely on a simple concept: the future depends on the present, not the history. This idea is known as the **Markov property** or **memorylessness**
