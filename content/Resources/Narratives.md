What is a narrative?
Narrative isnt just some of active expression that has a point. An artifact that is a story has a structure to it. "Narrative is the representation of an event or a series of events" Porter 2008. Something that happened, sequence of events, different then a question or a statement, a poem or a painting is not a narrative. 
**Narrative**: story + discourse
**Story**: plot + space
**Plot**: events, ordered in time
**Space**: characters, settings, props
**Discourse**: a particular telling of the story (style, ordering, duration, focalizations)
POV is key to the discourse.

Theories of plot
Joseph Campbell's mono-myth
Freytag's pyramid: exposition --> rising action --> climax --> falling action --> denouement

Critique of structuralism

Speculative Structuralism - we propose what a narrative looks like and we implement in a system of rules (a computer program).
