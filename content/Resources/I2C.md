I2C (Inter-Integrated Circuit) is ==a synchronous, multi-master, multi-slave, serial communication protocol used to connect low-speed peripherals to microcontrollers using only two wires: Serial Data (SDA) and Serial Clock (SCL)==. It uses 7-bit or 10-bit addressing, supports up to 1008 devices, and operates at speeds up to 5 Mbps.

Two wires to talk to potentially many devices — that's the beauty of I2C. `SCL` is the **clock** (keeps both sides in sync) and `SDA` is the **data** line.
