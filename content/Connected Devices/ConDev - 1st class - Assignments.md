Connected Devices Around Me:
- Take note of all the electronic devices you interact with during your week. Not just personal devices, like laptops or phones, but also public ones, like card readers, motion sensors, ATM or point-of-sale keypads, and anything else you notice. Note also the ones that affect your environment indirectly. Write them down and we’ll discuss them next week.

The most interesting one is a mobile air-quality monitoring unit operated by NYC Department of Environmental Protection (DEP). Used to measure things like: particulate matter (PM2.5 / PM10), gases (NO₂, CO, ozone — depending on configuration), sometimes temperature & humidity (for calibration). I love this object and I love its design. Box, wheels, sensor mast (pole), lock. Has huge NYC on it.
