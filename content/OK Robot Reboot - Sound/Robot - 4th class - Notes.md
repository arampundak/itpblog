Robot & Art
Art that can be more then art, an automatons where a part of "time machines" or "machine art". Tatlin's "Babylon Tower" sparked the movement of showing brutal mechanical creation as art. Moma machine art - art becoming machine or machines making art, can we elevate design to be art. The start of Design and Architectural wing in Moma, can we make mass produced objects elevate to be art. A lot of metal work, precision. In 1938 Bruno Munari published the "Manifesto Del Machina". He established the idea of machine art and imperfection, make machine more then just for production - to be put in galleries. Munari made the firsts kinetic art (together with Calder). He said artist should make machines as well and also supported being designers [[Design as Art - Bruno Munari]].
