**Retrospective of Non Human Automatons**
The word **“robot”** comes from the Czech language and was first introduced in a 1921 play by **Karel Čapek**, titled **R.U.R. (Rossum’s Universal Robots)**. It derives from the Czech word _robota_, meaning forced labor or drudgery.
