**Speech Recognition**
Dictaphone (1907) - Reduce the need of having a stenographer (someone taking notes).  
Audry (1952) - Designed at Bell Labs, could recognize spoken digits.
Shoebox (1961) - William C Dersh, Instructed an adding machine to calculate and print answers to simple arithmetic problems. Breaking sound to small pieces.
Hearsay (1973) - Initiated in DARPA, was one of the first systems capable of continuous speech recognition. Combining linguistic researchers and CS engineers.
Harpy (1976) - The start of talk based interaction with machine. One of the first usages of Speech to Text. Giving commands not so much of conversations.

Design challenges:
Expectations  - a robot voice might make us expect less of a machine while a more normalized voice might make us expect things the bot can't do. 
Accuracy - How do you know if someone finished talking or just taking a pause? 
Privacy - The right to withdrawal - having a hard switch to shutdown a machine.
Solving:
- Confirmation loop - checking to see if a softbot understand what you mean, repeating and confirming - "did i get this right?". Also not repeating but just checking with the user - Julia SNL. You can ask specifi things and not repeat the whole sentence.
- Breaking a conversation
- Checking
