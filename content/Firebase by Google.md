### What Firebase is for

Before Firebase, your page only lived in one person's browser. When you refresh, everything is gone, and nobody else sees it. Firebase gives you a **server you control from client-side JavaScript**, so you don't have to write any backend code. It solves two problems:

- **Persistence.** Data survives refreshes and outlasts your session.
- **Sharing.** Other people see the same data. Firestore can also push changes live to every open browser, which is how the moving picture updated on his second machine.
