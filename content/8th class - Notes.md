### How to communicate your idea before its done

**Storyboards!**
Using an abstracted storyboard can help with understanding how people relate to an idea. 
which are also Shoot-list

**Animatics**
Story board in sequence, to know what to have or not to have.

**Schematics & Diagrams**
For system build or for communicating the project before its done.
Dialog flow, Decision Tree

**Renders & Visualisations**
Show renders to sell your idea before its real.

**Style Frames**
After documentation and taking a snap shot out of it. Small frames of mid movement. For designing the style. Making the same frame in different styles - vikings in very 2D, vikings in rounded shapes, vikings in a cartoonish way. Design only a few frames that looks like its part of a complete sequence, but its actually not. To figure out the look.
