## Tip of the Week

The process of designing and developing projects can be quite overwhelming, especially when dealing with complex topics.  
One tip to find the right scope for your project and the way to frame it is to envision the same project in the extremes.

- How can I develop this project with $10.000 in 10 months?
- How can I develop this project with $10 in 10 days?

Establishing those scenarios can spark new and interesting ways to frame your ideas. These decisions can help you reach a thoughtful balance for the size and scope of the final object that can effectively convey your message.
