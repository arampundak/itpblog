Communicating the Idea
Communicating, mapping and showing your ideas.
Do you know this feeling of watching a movie after reading the book?
While reading the book you are the creator of the world, in your imagination.
While watching a movie - many people took decisions for you. from soundtrack design to coloring to what actors will appear.

When people start a project they think it in a linear progression. Most of the times its not like that. A tool to explore progression is:

**Mind Map** 
is a tool, not a framework
While writing a mind map, a branch can be Creative Ideas, then inside another branch can be Communication device - metaphors, analogies. Interface, Mood.
==Write down the first thing that comes in mind - it might clear space for new ideas. Or show the ideas behind the first thoughts.==

**Mood Board**
Is to help with the mood. Giving a feel, a mood. Its a generalist. Its a format.
How does one show interaction design - Maybe with a picture of a mouth, of hand touching. 
Talking about scale, not giving specific but giving general mood of.
Is it inside or outside, winter or summer, what sensation is there?
Collectively or by yourself?
