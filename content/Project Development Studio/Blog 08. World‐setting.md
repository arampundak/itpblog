## Talking wood

I had a technical talk with Ian and Niko about fabricating the shell in wood, and how to break the structure apart so it can actually be manufactured. We went over the CNC options at ITP and what would need to be outsourced.

What I took from it:
- **5-axis CNC** - very expensive.
- **The basement CNC** - means very small parts and thin material.
- **Veneer laminated over 3D prints** - both of them suggested it. The print is the form, the veneer is the wood.
- **Wood bends on one axis only** - so the shell has to be made of simple geometric surfaces, no compound curves.

That last one is a real design constraint, and a useful one. It makes me wonder if the answer to boxy vs. rounded is somewhere in between: flat faces joined by soft, single-axis bends. I'll take it into the final design. The schedule is not in my favor.

## In which world does Ari live?

Ari isn't set in a far future. It lives in the ITP shop, next to the laser cutter, on an evening when the staff are busy with someone else. The world I want to show is the present, while making people understand the future is near.
