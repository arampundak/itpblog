>“Nonverbal Interactions with Soli Radar” we explore how the combination of new sensing and machine learning techniques can be used to capture the submillimeter motion of our fingers to create expressive and subtle hand gestures to interact with a variety products.

Google ATAP’s (Advanced Technology and Projects) Soli radar research explores ambient, camera-free human-computer interaction (HCI) that reads nonverbal cues, spatial awareness, and body language using miniature millimeter-wave radar
