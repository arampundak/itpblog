On January 19, 2024, 207 years after the epic feat of the Crossing of the Andes that consolidated the liberation of the peoples of South America, a revolutionary robot, along with its insurgent army of artists, engineers and guides, recreated the historic Sanmartinian odyssey in search of their liberation.

For the first time in human history, a terrestrial exploration robot crossed the Andes, traversing peaks over 4,500 meters high, with the mission of establishing a technological practice of resistance determined to break with modern models of innovation based on violence, oppression, and exploitation. Guanaquerx is an artistic-technological project, a collective and emancipatory performance conceived from an “Andean cosmotechnics” that illuminates possible futures for all humans and non-humans who cohabit the planet.
