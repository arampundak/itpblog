LLM summery
**“Personality in Design”** argues that design inevitably communicates _personality_, whether designers intend it or not. Through tone, wording, visual choices, interaction patterns, and behavior, products express attitudes like friendliness, authority, playfulness, or coldness. The article emphasizes that personality isn’t decoration—it’s part of how users understand, trust, and emotionally relate to a system. ==When personality is inconsistent, accidental, or mismatched to context, it creates confusion and friction==. Thoughtful designers should therefore **intentionally design personality** so it aligns with the product’s purpose, audience, and values.
