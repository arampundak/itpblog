Donna Haraway's "A Cyborg Manifesto" (1985) is ==a foundational text in feminist theory, science and technology studies, and posthumanism that uses the cyborg—a hybrid of machine and organism—as a metaphor to challenge traditional dualisms like human/animal, nature/culture, and male/female==. It critiques essentialist feminism, advocating for a politics of affinity and coalition over identity, and embraces the hybrid, partial, and ironic nature of the cyborg to build a more inclusive future, moving beyond rigid categories and origin stories.
