A reference Pedro suggested. She’s an Argentine artist working in robotics, sculpture and performance. Makes artistic robotic projects that are somewhat different.

Made Guanaquerx
![[ref - Guanaquerx_GaetanoAdi_2_preview.webp]]

>For the first time in the history of humanity a land exploration robot crossed the Andes mountains navigating peaks above 15,000 feet with the ultimate mission of establishing emancipatory technological practices that resists the unmaking of our planet. A future-oriented declaration, Guanaquerx is both multi-day performance/expedition and a collective artistic feat aimed at promoting an 'andean cosmotechnics' that fosters the conditions for all earth-beings to flourish.
