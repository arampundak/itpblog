Socratic instruction is ==a form of question-driven, dialogic teaching based on the classical method of the philosopher Socrates==. Instead of lecturing or handing out direct facts, the instructor guides students using open-ended, probing questions to examine beliefs, analyze complex ideas, and discover conclusions independently.

Try asking your AI to talk to you with Socratic instruction.

**History:**
Socrates's most famous student was Plato, who went on to teach Aristotle who taught Alexander the Great.
All around 350 BCE.
