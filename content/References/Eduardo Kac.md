His work explores the intersection of biology, technology, and communication, often using genetic engineering and robotics to create "transgenic" organisms that challenge traditional boundaries between human and non-human life.
 >Key Works and Concepts
>- **GFP Bunny (2000):** Perhaps his most famous work, it involved the creation of **Alba**, a rabbit genetically engineered with a green fluorescent protein (GFP) from a jellyfish, which caused her to glow green under blue light.
>- **Genesis (1999):** An installation that translated a biblical verse into Morse code and then into DNA base pairs, which were implanted into bacteria. Participants online could activate a UV light to cause the bacteria to mutate, metaphorically altering the "text".
>- **Time Capsule (1997):** Kac became the first human to have a **digital microchip implanted** in his body for an artwork, addressing the fusion of digital memory with biological systems.
>- **Natural History of the Enigma (2003–2008):** Featured the "**Edunia**," a genetically engineered petunia that expresses the artist's own DNA in its red veins.
>- **Holopoetry:** Since 1983, Kac has developed "holographic poetry," where language is liberated from the printed page and floats in space, changing as the viewer moves.
