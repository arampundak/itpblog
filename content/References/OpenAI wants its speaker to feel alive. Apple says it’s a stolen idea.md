Summery by AI
This Fortune piece (July 15, 2026) covers OpenAI's first hardware device and Apple's lawsuit over it.

**The device:** Citing Bloomberg's sources, it describes a screen-free smart speaker meant to act as a new kind of home computer, using ChatGPT to control home appliances, play media, respond to messages, and answer questions. It's meant to become more personalized and proactive over time by drawing on data like the user's emails, and it has self-moving mechanical parts so it feels alive rather than like an object following commands. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/15/openai-building-human-like-chatgpt-device-apple-jony-ive/?utm_campaign)[Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/15/openai-building-human-like-chatgpt-device-apple-jony-ive/?utm_campaign)

**The market:** AI-first home devices are still early. Google's new Gemini speaker got mixed reviews: its conversations feel more natural, but it ignores some questions and gets confused. The closest comparison is ElliQ from Intuition Robotics, a lamp-like companion for older adults with moving parts. It prompts users with jokes and questions during the day and tracks wellness data and appointments. Humane's AI Pin is the cautionary tale: it was criticized for slow performance and overheating, and HP shut it down after acquiring the startup for $116 million. [OpenAI wants its speaker to feel alive. Apple says it's a stolen idea | Fortune +2](https://fortune.com/2026/07/15/openai-building-human-like-chatgpt-device-apple-jony-ive/?utm_campaign)

**The lawsuit:** Apple sued OpenAI and two former Apple employees, alleging OpenAI used their insider knowledge to build its hardware division. Apple claims OpenAI asked them in interviews about secret projects and asked them to bring prototypes. OpenAI says it has no interest in other companies' trade secrets. **Context:** OpenAI bought Jony Ive's studio io for $6.5 billion last year and has filed to go public. Altman wants a family of products to replace smartphones and computers, arguing that current hardware was designed for a world without AI. There's no release date, though OpenAI said in January it would unveil the device in the second half of 2026.
