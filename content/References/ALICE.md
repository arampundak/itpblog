**A.L.I.C.E.** (**Artificial Linguistic Internet Computer Entity**), also referred to as **Alicebot**, or simply **Alice**, is a [natural language processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing "Natural language processing") [chatbot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot "Chatbot")—a program that engages in a conversation with a human by applying some heuristical pattern matching rules to the human's input. It was inspired by [Joseph Weizenbaum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Weizenbaum "Joseph Weizenbaum")'s classical [[ELIZA]] program.
