Chicago Pile-1 (CP-1) was ==the world's first artificial, self-sustaining nuclear chain reaction==, achieved on December 2, 1942, in a converted squash court at the University of Chicago. Led by [Enrico Fermi](https://www.google.com/search?q=Enrico+Fermi&rlz=1C5OZZY_enUS1147US1148&oq=chicago+pile&gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUqDQgAEAAYkQIYgAQYigUyDQgAEAAYkQIYgAQYigUyCQgBEEUYORiABDIMCAIQABgUGIcCGIAEMgwIAxAAGBQYhwIYgAQyBwgEEAAYgAQyBwgFEAAYgAQyBwgGEAAYgAQyBwgHEAAYgAQyBwgIEAAYgAQyBwgJEAAYgATSAQgyNTcxajBqN6gCALACAA&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8&mstk=AUtExfAv4kdBZBibQTCHCUZZofWkDMNXTJNOHWYmIqhTloVtskdJ5U-xPTwk-Ieju0n_LH_yG85rgopgcTUYIM97yLUZv7YihEMQA5cRlyGaVcNf_tCMKxAWp_dEhvDrIoauleqZl4EXtmHSVWuhQFiN-WDjQHpbn0BdgyzSJymteksmRpRQC9BLCjRO8ucZTWpOhh814vss8XKVhmsqPjQfX272-AwjX4YWf4Jt3MRegoa7Vj7zAN82ybJ0yoQx28zqjmwZ7ZuYg0s3omhlnSr8H1tIeMSxncxSE_F7oBrcNBzc_xjb-6_c9-Jcmw9KQr19UQ&csui=3&ved=2ahUKEwiMlPbKy_CSAxX2lokEHeBVMwcQgK4QegQIARAE) as part of the Manhattan Project, this crucial experiment proved that controlled nuclear energy was possible, laying the groundwork for the atomic bomb and nuclear power. 

**Key Details of Chicago Pile-1:**

- **Significance:** It proved the feasibility of a nuclear chain reaction, enabling the development of the atomic bomb and the Atomic Age.
- **Construction:** It was a 20-foot-tall, roughly spherical pile made of 770,000 pounds of graphite blocks, with uranium and uranium oxide embedded inside.
- **Mechanism:** The pile used cadmium control rods to absorb neutrons and manage the reaction speed.
- **Operation:** On Dec. 2, 1942, the reactor achieved criticality—a self-sustaining reaction—with power levels barely enough to light a light bulb.
- **Location:** Built under the west stands of Stagg Field at the University of Chicago.
- **Legacy:** The reactor was later moved and reassembled as CP-2 at the Argonne National Laboratory. The original site is now a National Historic Landmark.
