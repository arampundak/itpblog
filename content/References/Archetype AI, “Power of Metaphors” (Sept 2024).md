- It sorts AI into three metaphors. In the Companion, agency is shared. In the Toolkit, the user keeps agency. In the Enchanted Object, the AI operates in the background, anticipating needs and acting on inferred intent, and holds most of the agency.[](https://www.archetypeai.io/blog/shaping-human-ai-interaction)

- They note that current physical AI mostly borrows the companion metaphor, and your own description (“companionable, empathic”) defaults to it too. Your interest in concealment and consent is really a challenge to the Enchanted Object model, where sensing is invisible by design. You could make that tension explicit.
