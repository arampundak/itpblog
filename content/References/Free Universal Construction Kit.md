The _[Free Universal Construction Kit](http://fffff.at/free-universal-construction-kit)_ is a matrix of adapter bricks that enable complete interoperability between ten popular childrens construction toys. By allowing any piece to join to any other, the Kit encourages totally new forms of intercourse between otherwise closed systems. As with other grassroots interoperability remedies, the _Free Universal Construction Kit_ implements proprietary protocols in order to provide a public service unmet, or unmeetable, by corporate interests.
