a growing collection of artworks involving language-based simulated personas and conversations. initially compiled as research for [FOREVER MEETINGS](https://www.bitforms.art/exhibition/forever-meetings).   

# ARTWORKS

Organized into three categories: [Chatbots](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.1nwopbkhlpbw),  [Agents](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.9fgsag20q26r), and [Generative Convos](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.kda9hg9r0a7k). Of  course there are some overlaps, but I am distinguishing them here as: 

[CHATBOTS](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.1nwopbkhlpbw): Artworks where users are meant to interact with a bot generating language, or where conversation is performed with a chatbot.  

[AGENTS AS ART](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.9fgsag20q26r): Artworks where craft of the character (design, persona, etc) of an AI agent is an artform

[GENERATIVE CONVERSATIONS](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.kda9hg9r0a7k): Artworks where language generators are put in conversation with each other, where the focus is the conversation system.

[ARTISTS](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.djcybhregqaa): Artists that have been working with chatbots in various forms for over a decade - these artists' practices are relevant beyond a single work. 

[MISC](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K34DPJ3buk8imCscN8xKiXH16YA0GT-Pi_TyFwI_hBQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.foibdfjwd86a) - artworks that belong somewhere on this list…. but where?

  

A few parameters:

- of course the lines are a bit blurry, but I'm looking for artworks (not games or products)
- the primary *focus* of the work is the craft of the bot, agent, or conversation
- it should have decent enough documentation online, unless the work is older than 10 years then I'll accept whatever we can get!
- for now, this list excludes student work that hasn't been shown outside of school contexts, though may in the future include a section of "student work"
