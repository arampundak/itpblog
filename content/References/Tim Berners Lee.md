Inventor of [[HTTP]]
Sir Timothy Berners Lee is a ==British computer scientist best known as the **inventor of the World Wide Web**==. In 1989, while working at **CERN**, he proposed a global hypertext project that would allow scientists to share information across different computer systems.

Key Inventions and Contributions

- **The World Wide Web (1989):** He developed the first web server, the first web browser (originally named WorldWideWeb), and the first version of the **Hypertext Markup Language (HTML)**.
- **Open Access:** He famously ensured that the technology for the web was made available **royalty-free** so that anyone could use it, which was critical to its rapid global adoption.
- **Standards and Leadership:** In 1994, he founded the **World Wide Web Consortium (W3C)** at MIT to oversee the development of web standards. He is also the founder of the [World Wide Web Foundation](https://webfoundation.org/) and the Open Data Institute.
