> **Dr. Kate Darling** is a leading expert in **robot ethics, technology policy, and human-robot interaction**.
> She is a research scientist at the [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/people/kdarling/overview/) and the head of the Ethics & Society research team at the Boston Dynamics AI Institute. Her work focuses on the emotional connections humans form with lifelike machines and how these relationships impact legal, social, and economic policies. 
> She is best known for her book, _The New Breed: What Our History with Animals Reveals About Our Future with Robots_ (2021). In it, she argues that rather than fearing an AI apocalypse or treating robots like humans, we should look to our historical relationship with domesticated animals as a blueprint for coexisting with autonomous technology.
