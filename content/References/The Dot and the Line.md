_**The Dot and the Line: A Romance in Lower Mathematics**_ is a 1965 animated short film.

The story details a straight blue [line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line_\(mathematics\) "Line (mathematics)") who is hopelessly in love with a red [dot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_\(geometry\) "Point (geometry)"). The dot, finding the line to be stiff, dull, and conventional, turns her affections toward a wild and unkempt squiggle. Taking advantage of the line's stiffness, the squiggle rubs it in that he is a lot more fun for the dot.
