**Reachy Mini** is an ==11-inch-tall, open-source desktop companion robot== developed by [Pollen Robotics](https://pollen-robotics.com/) in collaboration with **Hugging Face** and manufactured by Seeed Studio. Designed for AI experimentation, creative coding, and human-robot interaction, it serves as a highly expressive physical avatar for LLMs and vision-language models.
