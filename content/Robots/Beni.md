**Beni** is an autonomous, two-wheeled, bipedal **all-terrain camera robot** developed by [Mondo Robotics](https://www.mondorobotics.com/)Designed primarily for creators, solo vloggers, families, and pet owners, it acts as a ground-level, autonomous camera operator that tracks and films subjects without needing a traditional camera crew or dealing with strict drone regulations.
