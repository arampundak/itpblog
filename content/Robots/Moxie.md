Moxie is an AI-powered social companion robot by Embodied, Inc. Designed by [[Fuseproject]]. to help children (aged 5–10) develop social, emotional, and cognitive skills through play-based learning. It uses machine learning to recognize faces, emotions, and natural speech to create, personalized, engaging conversations. However, following an abrupt company shutdown, the robots ceased functionality in late 2024, leaving users with non-functional units.
