We believe robots should be expressive and full of life, not just minimalist shells and monochrome screens. Coglet is a robotics kit for those of us who grew up with WALL-E, R2-D2 and Baymax - robots that communicated through movement, mechanics, and personality.

Coglet combines intricate mechanical design, expressive animation, and modern electronics into a single maker kit. Unlike other screen-based assistants, Coglet moves, reacts, and behaves with life and personality. 

The Coglet project is also a platform which allows makers to design their own robots combining cloud applications like LLMs and image processing with motors, audio and sensors.
