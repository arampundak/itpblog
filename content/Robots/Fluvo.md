>Fluvo is a companion robot designed around emotional expression, helping children whose emotional empathy has been weakened by instruction-based moral education to personally feel and understand “goodness” through everyday interactions. For children aged 5–10 who often struggle with recognizing and considering others’ emotions, the robot expresses different emotional reactions in response to the child’s actions. Through repeated experiences of responding to and restoring these emotional cues, the child naturally learns empathy, care, and positive moral behavior.
