Core Content & Topics

- **Artistic Research:** Explores what artistic research means and how it links to the creative and making process.
- **Practical Steps:** Guides readers through finding a topic, formulating a research question, and developing arguments.
- **Writing & Structure:** Covers rhetorical text structuring, choosing writing styles, and deploying quotations effectively.
- **Multidisciplinary Materials:** Addresses the role of visual, auditory, spatial, virtual, and embodied materials in research documents like essays, theses, and proposals.
- **Real-World Examples:** Features dozens of examples from art and design to assist creators in building their own documentation.
