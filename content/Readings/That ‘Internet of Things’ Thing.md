Kevin Ashton argues that the Internet of Things is about letting computers sense the physical world directly, instead of relying on humans to input data. Because people are poor at accurately capturing information about real-world objects, computers end up knowing more about ideas than things. By using sensors and RFID, IoT can reduce waste, improve efficiency, and better align digital systems with the physical world that actually matters.
